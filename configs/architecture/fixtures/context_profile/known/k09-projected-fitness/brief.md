# FitLoop: platform rebuild brief

## From our investor deck

FitLoop serves 250,000 active members and processes 2 million workout logs a month. Members connect their watches, log every session and get a weekly plan from our coaching engine. FitLoop is the fastest-growing coaching app in North America.

## Where we really are

The figures above are from our Series A deck and are our targets for the end of next year, not today's numbers. Today we have about 3,800 paying members, who log about 41,000 workouts a month. Sign-ups in January run at about three times a normal month, because of New Year resolutions. We launched eighteen months ago and members pay $12 a month.

The app syncs heart rate, sleep and workout data from Apple Watch and Garmin devices. Members log workouts offline and the app syncs later, so a short outage of our servers is tolerable; losing workouts a member has already logged is not. We keep workout history for as long as the account is open. Members are in the US, Canada and the UK.

## Rules we have agreed to

We use Apple HealthKit, and its rules forbid using health data for advertising; we have agreed to them and will not break them. Our terms promise members that they can export and delete all of their data at any time, and that promise stays.

## Team and money

We are seven engineers: four on the mobile apps, two on the Python backend, and one on data. Nobody is on call at night yet. Our cloud bill is $6,500 a month. The board has capped this rebuild at $80,000.

## Priorities

Our first priority is cutting the cloud bill. Second, shipping the coach-chat feature by spring. Third, being ready for the growth in the deck when it comes.

## Open questions

Coach chat would let members message human coaches. We have not decided whether coaches will be employees or contractors, or in which countries they will work.
