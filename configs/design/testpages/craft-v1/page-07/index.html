How a class runs

Two hours, one skill at a time. You leave with clay under your nails and a piece on the drying shelf.

  1. 1

    Arrive and settle in

    Aprons, tools and tea are ready. Six people a class means there is room to ask anything.

  2. 2

    Watch, then try

    A short demonstration, then your hands in the clay with a teacher beside you.

  3. 3

    Shape now, trim later

    Pieces dry for a week before trimming. Nothing is wasted: spare clay is recycled.

  4. 4

    Glaze and collect

    Choose from twelve house glazes. We fire everything and send a message when it is ready.

Questions

Do I need any experience?
None. Taster evenings and Wheel basics start from zero.
What should I wear?
Clothes you do not mind getting muddy. We lend aprons and towels.
Can I miss a week?
Tell us a day ahead and join the same session of the next course.
Is it suitable for children?
Classes are for ages 16 and up. Family afternoons run in school holidays.
