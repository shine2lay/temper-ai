Why teams switch from spreadsheets

Most small teams start with a spreadsheet and a group chat. It works until someone swaps a shift by text and nobody updates the sheet, or two people both think they are on the Saturday close, or payroll finds forty extra hours at the end of the month. Fernway keeps one rota that everyone sees on their phone, records every swap and every approval, and tells you the week's cost before you publish it, so the sheet and the chat stop disagreeing.

Setting up takes an afternoon. Import your team from a spreadsheet, set each person's roles and contracted hours, and copy in a typical week. From then on most managers spend about ten minutes a week on the rota, mostly approving swaps.
