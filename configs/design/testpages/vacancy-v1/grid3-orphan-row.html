Roomwise, layout fixture

Features

Live room status

See which rooms are free right now, on every floor.

Two-tap booking

Hold a room for the next half hour from your phone.

No-show release

Rooms nobody turns up for go back on the board.

Calendar sync

Bookings show up in the calendar your team already uses.

Room displays

A tablet by the door shows who has the room and until when.

Usage reports

Find out which rooms are too big, too small or never used.

Guest passes

Visitors get a pass by email and find their room on a map.
