Questions

What people ask before they book their first room.

Can I try Roomwise before I pay?

Yes. Every plan starts with a thirty-day trial, and you add a card only when you decide to stay.

Does it work with our calendar?

Roomwise syncs with the calendar your team already uses, both ways, within a minute.

What happens when nobody turns up?

After ten minutes without a check-in the room goes back on the board for someone else.

Can visitors book a room?

Visitors cannot book, but you can send them a guest pass that shows their room on a map.

Do we need tablets by every door?

No. Room displays are optional; the board on any phone or laptop is enough.
