Roomwise, layout fixture

One board for every room

The board lists every room in the office with who has it, until when, and how many people it seats.

Free rooms are at the top. Tap one to hold it for the next half hour, or drag the end time to keep it longer.

Rooms with a display by the door show the same board, so people in the corridor see it too.

When a meeting ends early, the person who booked it can give the room back with one tap.

Office managers can close a room for cleaning or repairs, and every booking in it moves to a free room.

The board works on any phone or laptop, with no app to install and nothing to keep up to date.

Guests get a pass by email that opens the front door and shows them the way to their room.

Each evening the board sends office managers a short note of rooms that were booked but never used.

Recurring meetings release their room on their own when nobody checks in for three weeks in a row.

Everything on the board can be read aloud by a screen reader, and every action works from the keyboard.
