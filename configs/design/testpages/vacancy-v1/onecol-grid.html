Security

Your bookings stay yours

Room names, bookings and guest passes are stored in one region you choose, and they are encrypted on disk and on the wire.

People sign in with the work account they already have. When someone leaves, their access ends the moment their account is closed.

Every change to a room or a booking is kept in a log that office managers can read and export at any time.

We never sell or share booking data, and we delete it within thirty days of the day you close your account.
