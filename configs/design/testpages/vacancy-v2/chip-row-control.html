About Roomwise

Roomwise started in a shared office where four teams fought over two meeting rooms and a spreadsheet nobody kept up to date.

We built a board that shows every room at once, a calendar that books them in two taps and a display by the door that tells the next team when to come in.

Today Roomwise runs meeting rooms in dental practices, law firms, schools and shared offices, from one room to a few hundred.

The company is twelve people in two cities, and every one of them answers the support line at least one day a month.
