Roomwise, layout fixture

Rooms

Roomwise treats every space as a room: the big boardroom, the two-person phone booth, the quiet corner by the window and the desk a visitor borrows for an afternoon.

Each room has a name, a size, the things in it and the hours it can be booked, and the board shows all of them at once so nobody walks the floor looking for a free one.

Rooms can be grouped by floor or by building, and a group can be hidden from visitors or opened to the whole company.

When a meeting ends early the room goes back on the board at once, and when nobody turns up it is released after ten minutes.

Boardroom

Twelve seats, a wall screen and a speakerphone for the whole team.

Phone booth

One seat, a door that closes and a socket for a laptop.

Window corner

Four soft chairs and a low table for a relaxed catch-up.
