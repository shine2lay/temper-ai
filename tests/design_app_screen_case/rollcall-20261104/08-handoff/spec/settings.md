Backend fields these need are listed under "Backend needs (#196)".

## What it is

One screen for the account and everything RollCall is allowed to do. First view: the account (net liq and the three figures behind it, margin cover), the Orders on/off switch, and where alerts go. The other ten sections are tiles, each with a one-line summary of its current values, that open in place. Nothing else is open on arrival.

Today's page shows all eleven panels open at once, with a section menu and one Save button at the top. Here: the important three first, the rest behind tiles, and one sticky bar that appears only once something is edited.

## Layout

- Desktop 1440: header from more.js (back to More, eyebrow "More · Account and setup", h1 "Settings"). Account card full width; Orders and Notifications cards side by side; heading "Everything else"; tiles two to a row. An open section's panel spans the full width under its row of two, with a small notch pointing at its tile, a title and a Close button.
- Phone 390 and 320: top bar "Settings" with back. Cards stacked; tiles one per row; an open panel sits right under its tile (the tile shows as pressed). Every target is at least 44 px.
- Type: body 16, labels 14, eyebrows 14 uppercase; at most one solid primary on screen (Turn on orders, Connect tastytrade, or the save bar's Save / Try again; never two at once).

One row per backend channel, in this order and with these names:

| Row | Backend id | Description line | Setup keys (write-only) |
|---|---|---|---|

Each row (except In the app): name + chip (First choice / Backup / Every alert / Off / Not set up), switch on the right, a "Use as" select (First choice, Backup, Copy of every alert) while on, and a "Setup  N of M saved" button that opens its keys. Keys are write-only, as in Connection: saved → "Saved · ends 4f2a" pill (last four only; a password or secret shows just "Saved") + "Replace" (opens an empty password box); not saved → password box with "Not set" or "Optional". The key itself is never shown back.
Mapping: First choice → `alerts.primary`; Backup → `alerts.fallback`; Copy of every alert → in `alerts.always[]`; Off → in none. `inapp` always stays in `alerts.always`.

**Send a test, per channel.** Every channel that is set up (on or off, so it can be tried before turning it on; always for In the app) has a ghost "Send a test" button in its row (aria-label "Send a test to Telegram"). Click → the button shows a spinner and "Sending…" and the row says "Sending a test to Telegram…" at once; then "✓ Test sent just now · check Telegram" (ntfy "check your phone", Pushover "check the Pushover app", Email "check your inbox", In the app "look at the bell"), or "✕ The test didn't arrive." + the channel's own reason and what to do: ntfy "ntfy.sh didn't answer within 10 seconds. Replaces #185's single whole-route test.

Field "Send the same alert at most once every … min" (`alerts.dedupe_minutes`).

**Your own alarms** (AlarmsRegister) with a count. Each alarm: its words ("AAPL falls under $220") + info tip, a value line "Now $228.40 · $8.40 (3.8%) over the line" (the stock's price against the alarm's level, from the same positions data as Today), chip Watching / "Went off 9:58 AM", its note ("Add 100 shares"), and Remove ("Removing…" → gone; failed: "Couldn't remove it. **Rules** (RulesPanel, UpRulesGrid, EntryRules). "When to roll up" + info tip: the 17 rules of `rules.roll.up_rules` drawn as a map, rows = days left (Expiry day 0 days, Last days 1–2, Week out 3–7, Two weeks 8–14, Month out 15–35, Quarter out 36–120, Long-dated 121+), columns = cushion (under 10%, 10–25%, 25% or more); the first two rows are one wide box "any cushion". Each box: step-up % ("+2.0%") and new delta ("Δ 0.70"), tinted darker as the % grows. Tap a box → editor under the map with all ten columns of that rule (From, To, Cushion from, Cushion under, Step up when the stock is, New call's delta, Give or take, May go above the stock, Look out at most, Must pay at least) and "Done". Then eight disclosures, each with a two-value summary: When to roll down, Where a roll goes, Price cushion, New or barely covered, Cushion still building, Is time paying?, New positions, Which way it has been going (field paths in `fixtures/settings.json` `rule_groups`). "Restore the defaults" fills every rule with its default as drafts (still needs Save).

**Every rule number has an info tip** (the (i) after its label; in the roll-up editor, one per column): a plain one-line meaning, then "**Example**" in the book's own numbers (`fixtures/settings.json` `rule_help`, keyed by config path; `up:<column>` for the roll-up columns). Examples: New call's delta "The delta the new, higher call aims for. Higher sits closer to the money and pays more." / "Last days aims at 0.65: XOM's new call would sit a little under XOM's $114.60."; Roll down when delta falls below "When a call's delta drops under this, the stock has fallen away from the strike and the call earns little, so RollCall suggests rolling down." / "PFE's $26 call is at 0.28 delta, under 0.30, so RollCall suggests rolling it down."; Cushion from "AAPL is 10.1% over its $207.45 basis, so it reads the 10–25% column." The two retired fields (`near_strike_days`, `friday_near_strike_pct`) say "Retired: nothing reads this any more" and which rule does the job. Numbers in examples must come from the live book when built (positions, basis after premium, delta, days left), not be fixed text. Note for Frontend: the roll-up map measures cushion as price over basis (`spot / basis − 1`), the cushion tiers as a share of price (`(spot − basis) / spot`), so AAPL reads 10.1% in one and 9.2% in the other; the tips say "over its basis" in both. Keep each example on its own rule's measure.

## Behaviours

- **Stop switch** saves at the click (`PUT /api/settings` `{trading: {enabled}}`), no Save bar. Stopping is one tap, no question: "Stopping every order…" → "Stopped. Tickets won't send; orders already at tastytrade stay there." and the switch reads "Orders are stopped · Stopped just now · starting again asks first". Starting asks first, in a box inside the card: "Start orders again?" / "Tickets can send orders to your tastytrade account again. Nothing goes out until you press Send on a ticket." with "Start orders" (primary) and "Keep stopped". Failed: switch goes back, error line under it. Empty: disabled, "Connect a broker first, then turn orders on."
- **Arm real money** (`PUT /api/settings` `{trading: {live_enabled}}`). The words "real money" appear only in this control (and its Connection link), never as an account badge elsewhere. Step 1, "Step 1 of 2 · Arm real money? Here's what changes": "Orders you press Send on go to your tastytrade account ending 0412 and spend real money.", "The brakes still hold every order: up to $0.25 off the mid, 10 contracts an order.", "Stopping orders above still halts everything at once.", "Nothing is sent now. Disarming is one tap." + Continue / Cancel. Step 2, "Step 2 of 2 · Type ARM to arm real money": a box "Type ARM"; "Arm real money" (primary) stays disabled until it reads ARM. Then "Arming real money…" → "Armed. The next order you send spends real money." Failed: "Couldn't arm: RollCall's server didn't answer. Nothing changed; it's still not armed." Disarm is one tap: "Disarming…" → "Disarmed. No order reaches your tastytrade account ending 0412." Only one question shows at a time: opening the arm steps closes the start-orders confirm, and the other way round. Read-only server (`allow_live` false): "Real money: can't arm" / "This copy of RollCall was started read-only, so no order can reach your tastytrade account ending 0412.", the button disabled, "To allow it, restart RollCall with ROLLCALL_ALLOW_LIVE=1." and "See Connection" (opens Connection at the sending flag). Orders to Alpaca, the Practice broker or the tastytrade sandbox: "Nothing to arm: orders go to …, which never spends real money."
- **Send a test** (per channel) and **Scan now**: see Notifications and Screener above. Both show their pending state at the click, keep the rest of the page still, and end in a success line or a failure saying why and what to try.
- **Channel switch / Use as** save at the click: "Saving…" under the row, then "Saved" (or "Couldn't save. Try again." and the switch goes back). Scroll position and focus stay put. Turning on a channel that isn't set up opens its Setup and says what to add ("Add your user key and application token first, then turn it on."). Turning off the first choice makes the backup the first choice; with no backup it refuses: "Make another channel the first choice before turning this one off." Picking First choice for one channel moves the old first choice to Copy of every alert (or to backup if it was the backup's slot).
- **Every other field** is a draft: the field gets an "Edited" look, its tile and disclosure get "Edited", and a sticky bar appears at the bottom of the view: "N changes not saved" + the sections they're in, "Discard" and "Save" (primary). Save checks every draft first; a bad value opens its section, scrolls to the field, outlines it red and says why under it ("Enter a number", "Use a number from 0 to 240", "Use 0 or more", "Use whole hundreds: a contract covers 100 shares", "Act under must be lower than watch (1.5)", "Must be 3 or more", "Use a time like 15:30"); nothing is sent. Then "Saving…" (spinner) → "Saved" (check, fades after 2.4 s), sending only the changed paths (`PUT /api/settings` with the patch, keys to `PUT /api/settings/secrets`). Failed: red bar "Couldn't save" / "RollCall's server didn't answer. Your change is still here." with Discard and "Try again"; every draft stays in its box.
- **Info tips (i)** open a short help text under the label on tap; tap again to close.
- **Check now** (Schedule, Connection), **Send a test**, **Remove** (alarm): the button shows a spinner and its pending words at once ("Checking…", "Sending…", "Removing…"), then the result line; failures say what to try.
- Tiles toggle; only the tapped section opens or closes; the page keeps its scroll position.

## Backend needs (#196)

The design uses three things today's API doesn't give; until they exist, Frontend shows the fallback in brackets.

- **Last four of a saved key**: `GET /api/settings` returns only key names (`secret_keys`). Needs `secret_hints: {provider: {KEY: "4f2a"}}`, last four characters, computed on the server, never for keys marked secret/password (client secret, mail password, application token). [Fallback: "Saved" with no ending.]
- **Test one channel**: `POST /api/alerts/test` tests the whole route. Needs `?channel=<id>` that sends to that channel only, even when it's off, and returns its `AlertTestOut.channels[0]` with `ok` and a scrubbed `detail`. [Fallback: one "Send a test" for the route, as in #185.]
- **Scan now from Settings**: reuses `POST /api/screener/refresh` and the run's stages; no new endpoint. The account ending in the arm box needs the same last-four hint for `TASTYTRADE_ACCOUNT`. [Fallback: "your tastytrade account".]
