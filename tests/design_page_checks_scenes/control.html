How it works

Members pick up what they need at the Saturday counter and bring it back the next Saturday. Every tool is checked and cleaned by a volunteer before it goes back on the shelf.

Tools on the shelves

Four hundred tools, from a hedge trimmer to a tile cutter. Reserve one and it waits at the counter for you on Saturday morning.

What does a membership cost?
Twelve pounds a year, or nothing if you volunteer at the counter twice a year. The money pays for blades, batteries and the shed's rent.
What if I break something?
Tell us when you bring it back. Tools wear out; we only ask for a contribution when something was clearly misused, and we talk about it first.
Can I borrow more than one tool?
Up to three at a time. Large items such as the cement mixer and the scaffold tower are booked separately, because they need a car to collect.
Do you deliver?
No, but neighbours often pick up for each other. Write the other member's name on the reservation and the counter will hand the tool over.
Who runs the library?
Thirty volunteers from the neighbourhood, a small committee elected each spring, and a lot of borrowed shelving.
When is the counter open?
Saturdays from nine to one, and Wednesday evenings from six to eight in the summer months.
