# Interview: Rosa, chef-owner of a two-location taqueria

Fictional benchmark material. Date: 2026-08-12. Notes kept close to verbatim.

Q: How do you plan prep today?

Rosa: "Every morning I write the prep list on the whiteboard by the walk-in. I guess from what we sold last week."

Rosa: "On Sunday night I spend about two hours on next week's order in a spreadsheet."

Q: What happens to food you don't sell?

Rosa: "We throw out rice and beans almost every night. I write it in a notebook, but nobody reads the notebook."

Q: Have you tried software for this?

Rosa: "We tried Brigadeboard. It wanted a tablet at every station and it was $189 a month for one location."

Rosa: "I don't need inventory costing. I need to know how much carnitas to make on a Tuesday."

Q: After two weeks with Kettlemark, what changed?

Rosa: "The list is on my phone before I get to the kitchen. I just check it and print it."
