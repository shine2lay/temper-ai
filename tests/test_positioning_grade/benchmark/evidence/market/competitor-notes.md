# Competitor notes (sales team, August 2026)

Fictional benchmark material. All products named here are made up.

Brigadeboard: prep and station screens for restaurant kitchens. $189 per location per month. Needs a tablet at each station. Includes recipe costing.

Coldroom Suite: full inventory, costing and ordering for restaurant groups. $299 per location per month. Needs a weekly full count. Onboarding takes about six weeks.

Prep Sheets Club: free prep-list templates for spreadsheets. No sales data, no waste log.

In our sales calls, most kitchens use none of these. They use a whiteboard, a notebook or a group chat.
