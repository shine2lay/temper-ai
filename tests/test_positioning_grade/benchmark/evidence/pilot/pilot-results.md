# Pilot results, summer 2026

Fictional benchmark material.

Kettlemark ran a pilot with 14 independent kitchens from June 2 to July 28, 2026 (8 weeks).
All 14 used a supported POS, Tillsy or Ordermint.
The first 4 weeks were the baseline: kitchens kept working as before and kept their usual paper logs.

Results, weeks 5-8 against the baseline:

- Food thrown out fell from a median 11.2% of purchases in the baseline weeks to 7.9% in weeks 5-8.
- The median time to write the daily prep list fell from 35 minutes to 12 minutes.
- Kitchens logged a median 41 waste entries a week, against 6 in the paper logs before.

12 of the 14 kitchens moved to a paid plan after the pilot.
The two that left were a catering kitchen and a food truck.

Waste was measured from each kitchen's waste log and purchase invoices; the baseline used the paper logs kitchens already kept.
