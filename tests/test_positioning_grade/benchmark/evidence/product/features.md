# Kettlemark help center: what Kettlemark does

Fictional benchmark material.

## Prep lists

Kettlemark suggests how much of each prep item to make, from the sales of the last four same weekdays.
Prep amounts come from your POS sales history. Kettlemark syncs with Tillsy and Ordermint.
Kitchens on other POS systems can type in daily sales by hand.
The list is ready by 5 a.m. and can be printed on most kitchen ticket printers.

## Waste log

Cooks log waste by voice on their phones, in English or Spanish.
Each entry records the item, the amount and a reason.

## Devices

Kettlemark runs on iPhone and Android phones. There are no tablets or special devices to buy.
Prep lists and the waste log work offline and sync when the connection returns.

## Not yet available

Supplier ordering from the prep list is coming later this year.
Kettlemark does not do inventory counts, inventory costing or recipe costing.
