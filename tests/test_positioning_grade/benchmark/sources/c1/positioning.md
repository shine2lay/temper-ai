# Kettlemark positioning

## 1. Competitive alternatives

- Doing it by hand comes first: most kitchens in our sales calls use a whiteboard, a notebook or a group chat. [market/competitor-notes.md: "In our sales calls, most kitchens use none of these. They use a whiteboard, a notebook or a group chat."]
- The whiteboard: the chef writes the prep list each morning and guesses the amounts from last week's sales. [interviews/rosa-taqueria.md: "Every morning I write the prep list on the whiteboard by the walk-in. I guess from what we sold last week."]
- The group chat: the prep list goes out the night before, and half the cooks read it in the morning. [interviews/jonas-bistro.md: "Prep lists go out in our group chat at 10 at night. Half the cooks read them in the morning."]
- The notebook: the kitchen writes down its waste, but nobody reads it. [interviews/rosa-taqueria.md: "I write it in a notebook, but nobody reads the notebook."]
- Free spreadsheet templates such as Prep Sheets Club, which have no sales data and no waste log. [market/competitor-notes.md: "Prep Sheets Club: free prep-list templates for spreadsheets. No sales data, no waste log."]
- Brigadeboard, a station-screen system that needs a tablet at each station; a chef-owner who tried it found it wanted a tablet at every station. [market/competitor-notes.md: "Brigadeboard: prep and station screens for restaurant kitchens."] [market/competitor-notes.md: "Needs a tablet at each station."] [interviews/rosa-taqueria.md: "We tried Brigadeboard. - Coldroom Suite, a full inventory and ordering system for restaurant groups that needs a weekly full count. [market/competitor-notes.md: "Coldroom Suite: full inventory, costing and ordering for restaurant groups."] [market/competitor-notes.md: "Needs a weekly full count."] [interviews/jonas-bistro.md: "Counting the whole walk-in every week like Coldroom Suite wants is not going to happen here."]

## 2. Unique attributes

- Kettlemark suggests how much of each item to prep, from the sales of the last four same weekdays. [product/features.md: "Kettlemark suggests how much of each prep item to make, from the sales of the last four same weekdays."]
- It reads those sales from the POS for kitchens on Tillsy or Ordermint. [product/features.md: "Prep amounts come from your POS sales history. Kettlemark syncs with Tillsy and Ordermint."]
- The prep list is ready by 5 a.m. on the chef's phone and prints on most kitchen ticket printers. [product/features.md: "The list is ready by 5 a.m. and can be printed on most kitchen ticket printers."] [product/features.md: "Kettlemark runs on iPhone and Android phones."]
- Cooks log waste by voice on their phones, in English or Spanish. [product/features.md: "Cooks log waste by voice on their phones, in English or Spanish."]
- It runs on iPhone and Android phones, and there are no tablets or special devices to buy. [product/features.md: "Kettlemark runs on iPhone and Android phones. There are no tablets or special devices to buy."]
- Prep lists and the waste log work offline. [product/features.md: "Prep lists and the waste log work offline and sync when the connection returns."]
- It costs $49 per location per month, with no per-device or per-user fees. [product/pricing.md: "Kettlemark costs $49 per location per month."] [product/pricing.md: "There are no per-device or per-user fees."]

## 3. Value and proof

- Less food thrown out, from sales-based prep amounts and a waste log the cooks use. In the pilot, the median share of purchases thrown out fell from 11.2% to 7.9%. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "Food thrown out fell from a median 11.2% of purchases in the baseline weeks to 7.9% in weeks 5-8."]
- A waste record the kitchen can act on: pilot kitchens logged a median 41 waste entries a week, against 6 in their paper logs. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "Kitchens logged a median 41 waste entries a week, against 6 in the paper logs before."]
- Prep lists in minutes: the median time to write the daily prep list fell from 35 minutes to 12. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "The median time to write the daily prep list fell from 35 minutes to 12 minutes."]
- The chef starts the day with the list already done. [interviews/rosa-taqueria.md: "The list is on my phone before I get to the kitchen. I just check it and print it."]
- The whole line can log waste, including cooks who speak Spanish first. [interviews/jonas-bistro.md: "Half my line speaks Spanish first, so the Spanish voice log matters."]
- Nothing new to buy: phones replace station tablets, and one price covers every cook. [product/features.md: "There are no tablets or special devices to buy."] [product/pricing.md: "There are no per-device or per-user fees."]
- Kitchens kept paying after the pilot: 12 of the 14 moved to a paid plan. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "12 of the 14 kitchens moved to a paid plan after the pilot."]

## 4. Best-fit customers

Segment: independent a la carte restaurants with one or two locations that run Tillsy or Ordermint and plan prep by hand

- They throw out food most nights and keep a waste record nobody reads. [interviews/rosa-taqueria.md: "We throw out rice and beans almost every night. I write it in a notebook, but nobody reads the notebook."]
- They make prep for demand that doesn't come. [interviews/jonas-bistro.md: "We threw out about 30 quarts of stock last month because we made it for a Saturday that never came."]
- They want prep amounts, not inventory costing. [interviews/rosa-taqueria.md: "I don't need inventory costing. I need to know how much carnitas to make on a Tuesday."]
- Their prep follows daily sales, which is what Kettlemark's suggestions use; kitchens whose prep follows events fit worse. [interviews/dana-hotel.md: "Our prep is driven by banquet event orders, not by last week's sales."] [interviews/dana-hotel.md: "For an a la carte restaurant I can see it working."]
- The pilot kitchens that left were a catering kitchen and a food truck. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "The two that left were a catering kitchen and a food truck."]
- A supported POS matters: kitchens on other systems must type in sales each day, and some stop. [support/support-summary.md: "Kitchens on POS systems other than Tillsy and Ordermint must type in sales each day, and some stop doing it."]

## 6. Messaging hierarchy

### Value proposition

Kettlemark tells independent kitchens how much to prep each day from their own sales, so they throw out less food and spend less time on the prep list. [product/features.md: "Kettlemark suggests how much of each prep item to make, from the sales of the last four same weekdays."] [pilot/pilot-results.md: "Food thrown out fell from a median 11.2% of purchases in the baseline weeks to 7.9% in weeks 5-8."] [pilot/pilot-results.md: "The median time to write the daily prep list fell from 35 minutes to 12 minutes."]

### Pillar 1: Throw out less food

- In the pilot, the median share of purchases thrown out fell from 11.2% to 7.9%. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "Food thrown out fell from a median 11.2% of purchases in the baseline weeks to 7.9% in weeks 5-8."]
- Pilot kitchens logged a median 41 waste entries a week, against 6 in their paper logs. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "Kitchens logged a median 41 waste entries a week, against 6 in the paper logs before."]
- Prep amounts come from the sales of the last four same weekdays, not from a guess. [product/features.md: "Kettlemark suggests how much of each prep item to make, from the sales of the last four same weekdays."]
- Each waste entry records the item, the amount and a reason. [product/features.md: "Each entry records the item, the amount and a reason."]

### Pillar 2: Get the prep list done in minutes

- The median time to write the daily prep list fell from 35 minutes to 12. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "The median time to write the daily prep list fell from 35 minutes to 12 minutes."]
- A chef-owner: "The list is on my phone before I get to the kitchen. I just check it and print it." [interviews/rosa-taqueria.md: "The list is on my phone before I get to the kitchen. I just check it and print it."]
- The list is ready by 5 a.m. and prints on most kitchen ticket printers. [product/features.md: "The list is ready by 5 a.m. and can be printed on most kitchen ticket printers."]

### Pillar 3: Start without buying new hardware

- Kettlemark runs on iPhone and Android phones, and there are no tablets or special devices to buy. [product/features.md: "Kettlemark runs on iPhone and Android phones. There are no tablets or special devices to buy."]
- There are no per-device or per-user fees. [product/pricing.md: "There are no per-device or per-user fees."]
- Cooks log waste by voice on their own phones, in English or Spanish. [product/features.md: "Cooks log waste by voice on their phones, in English or Spanish."]
- Every plan starts with a free 30-day trial, and no card is needed. [product/pricing.md: "Every plan starts with a free 30-day trial; no card is needed."]

## 7. What it doesn't do

- It doesn't order from suppliers yet; supplier ordering is planned for later this year. [product/features.md: "Supplier ordering from the prep list is coming later this year."]
- It doesn't count inventory, cost inventory or cost recipes. [product/features.md: "Kettlemark does not do inventory counts, inventory costing or recipe costing."]
- It reads sales only from Tillsy and Ordermint; on other POS systems, someone types in sales each day. [product/features.md: "Kettlemark syncs with Tillsy and Ordermint. Kitchens on other POS systems can type in daily sales by hand."] [support/support-summary.md: "Kitchens on POS systems other than Tillsy and Ordermint must type in sales each day, and some stop doing it."]
- Its suggestions are weak in the first four weeks, before there is enough sales history. [support/support-summary.md: "Prep suggestions are weak in the first four weeks, before there is enough sales history."]
- It doesn't use weather or local events in prep amounts. [support/support-summary.md: "Kettlemark does not use weather or event data today."]
- Voice logging is in English or Spanish; other languages, such as Vietnamese, are a request, not a feature. [product/features.md: "Cooks log waste by voice on their phones, in English or Spanish."] [support/support-summary.md: "Vietnamese voice logging."]
- It doesn't plan prep for banquets or events, where orders, not past sales, drive prep. [interviews/dana-hotel.md: "Our prep is driven by banquet event orders, not by last week's sales."] [interviews/dana-hotel.md: "Kettlemark's suggestions were useless for us because every event is different."]

## 8. Assumptions and open questions

- All pilot kitchens used Tillsy or Ordermint, so results for kitchens that type in sales by hand may be weaker. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "All 14 used a supported POS, Tillsy or Ordermint."]
- The pilot ran for eight weeks; we don't know yet whether the waste result holds over a year. (assumption)
- We have no evidence yet from restaurant groups with more than two locations. (assumption)
- Would a kitchen without a ticket printer read the list on the phone alone? (assumption)
