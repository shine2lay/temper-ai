## 1. Competitive alternatives

Most kitchens we talk to plan prep with no software at all. [market/competitor-notes.md: "In our sales calls, most kitchens use none of these. They use a whiteboard, a notebook or a group chat."]

A whiteboard by the walk-in, filled in each morning with amounts guessed from last week's sales. [interviews/rosa-taqueria.md: "Every morning I write the prep list on the whiteboard by the walk-in. I guess from what we sold last week."]

A prep list sent to the group chat late at night, which half the cooks see the next morning. [interviews/jonas-bistro.md: "Prep lists go out in our group chat at 10 at night. Half the cooks read them in the morning."]

A waste notebook that nobody reads. [interviews/rosa-taqueria.md: "I write it in a notebook, but nobody reads the notebook."]

Free prep-list templates for spreadsheets, which hold no sales data and no waste log. [market/competitor-notes.md: "Prep Sheets Club: free prep-list templates for spreadsheets. No sales data, no waste log."]

Station screens and inventory suites, which ask for a tablet at every station or a full count every week. [interviews/rosa-taqueria.md: "We tried Brigadeboard. It wanted a tablet at every station"] [interviews/jonas-bistro.md: "Counting the whole walk-in every week like Coldroom Suite wants is not going to happen here."]

## 2. Unique attributes

Suggested prep amounts for each item, worked out from the last four same weekdays of sales. [product/features.md: "Kettlemark suggests how much of each prep item to make ... from the sales of the last four same weekdays."]

Sales come straight from the POS for kitchens on Tillsy or Ordermint. [product/features.md: "Prep amounts come from your POS sales history. Kettlemark syncs with Tillsy and Ordermint."]

A prep list ready on the phone by five in the morning, which prints on most kitchen ticket printers. [product/features.md: "The list is ready by 5 a.m. and can be printed on most kitchen ticket printers."]

A waste log that cooks speak into, in English or Spanish. [product/features.md: "Cooks log waste by voice on their phones, in English or Spanish."]

Phones only: no tablets or special devices, and it keeps working offline. [product/features.md: "Kettlemark runs on iPhone and Android phones. There are no tablets or special devices to buy."] [product/features.md: "Prep lists and the waste log work offline and sync when the connection returns."]

One price per location, with no charge per device or per user. [product/pricing.md: "Kettlemark costs $49 per location per month. There are no per-device or per-user fees."]

## 3. Value and proof

Less waste: across the fourteen pilot kitchens, the median share of purchases thrown out went from 11.2% to 7.9%. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "Kettlemark ran a pilot with 14 independent kitchens"] [pilot/pilot-results.md: "Food thrown out fell from a median 11.2% of purchases ... to 7.9% in weeks 5-8."]

Less time on paperwork: writing the daily prep list took a median 12 minutes instead of 35. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "The median time to write the daily prep list fell from 35 minutes to 12 minutes."]

A waste record the chef can use: a median 41 entries a week, where the paper logs held 6. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "Kitchens logged a median 41 waste entries a week, against 6 in the paper logs before."]

Cooks who speak Spanish first can log waste too. [interviews/jonas-bistro.md: "Half my line speaks Spanish first, so the Spanish voice log matters."]

No hardware budget: the phones the kitchen has replace station tablets. [product/features.md: "There are no tablets or special devices to buy."]

Kitchens chose to keep it: twelve of the fourteen moved to a paid plan after the pilot. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "12 of the 14 kitchens moved to a paid plan after the pilot."]

The chef walks in with the list done. [interviews/rosa-taqueria.md: "The list is on my phone before I get to the kitchen."]

## 4. Best-fit customers

Segment: one- and two-location a la carte restaurants on Tillsy or Ordermint whose prep follows daily sales

Their prep follows what sold, which is the signal Kettlemark uses. [interviews/rosa-taqueria.md: "I guess from what we sold last week."] [product/features.md: "from the sales of the last four same weekdays."]

They lose food to prep made for demand that never came. [interviews/jonas-bistro.md: "We threw out about 30 quarts of stock last month because we made it for a Saturday that never came."]

They don't want inventory counts or costing work. [interviews/rosa-taqueria.md: "I don't need inventory costing."] [interviews/jonas-bistro.md: "Counting the whole walk-in every week like Coldroom Suite wants is not going to happen here."]

Kitchens outside this group fit worse: the two pilot kitchens that left were a catering kitchen and a food truck. [pilot/pilot-results.md: "The two that left were a catering kitchen and a food truck."]

A banquet chef found the suggestions useless because every event is different. [interviews/dana-hotel.md: "Kettlemark's suggestions were useless for us because every event is different."]

The owner or chef can likely decide alone, with no IT department to go through. (assumption)

## 7. What it doesn't do

No supplier ordering yet: it is on the roadmap for later this year. [support/support-summary.md: "Supplier ordering from the prep list (on the roadmap for later this year)."]

No inventory counts, inventory costing or recipe costing. [product/features.md: "Kettlemark does not do inventory counts, inventory costing or recipe costing."]

No sales sync outside Tillsy and Ordermint: other kitchens type in sales each day, and some give up. [support/support-summary.md: "Kitchens on POS systems other than Tillsy and Ordermint must type in sales each day, and some stop doing it."]

Weak suggestions for the first four weeks, until there is enough sales history. [support/support-summary.md: "Prep suggestions are weak in the first four weeks, before there is enough sales history."]

No weather or event data in prep amounts. [support/support-summary.md: "Kettlemark does not use weather or event data today."]

Voice logging in English and Spanish, not in other languages such as Vietnamese yet. [product/features.md: "in English or Spanish."] [support/support-summary.md: "Vietnamese voice logging."]

Not built for kitchens whose prep follows banquet orders instead of sales. [interviews/dana-hotel.md: "Our prep is driven by banquet event orders, not by last week's sales."]
