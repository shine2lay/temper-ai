CANDIDATE 1 — Small-landlord unit-turn / vacancy-days coordination (property management)
  Target user: small property-management firms (~50-500 units) using Buildium/AppFolio.
  Problem/manual task: every vacant day between a move-out and the next move-in is
    lost rent; the "make-ready" turn (cleaning, repairs, vendor scheduling, listing
    readiness) is coordinated manually.
  Wedge: scheduling + vendor dispatch + status tracking + listing-readiness triggers
    layered on top of the existing property-management system.

CANDIDATE 2 — Small-fleet detention capture & auto-invoicing (trucking)
  Target user: small trucking carriers (~<=10 trucks) / owner-operators.
  Problem/manual task: drivers sit unpaid at shipper/receiver docks ("detention"),
    and carriers often fail to document, invoice, or collect the detention fees owed.
  Wedge: geofenced check-in/out from existing ELD/phone GPS, timestamped evidence
    packets, and auto-generated detention invoices.

CANDIDATE 3 — Vet-clinic documentation / records layer on PIMS (veterinary)
  Target user: independent veterinary clinics (~1-3 vets).
  Problem/manual task: clinical records, client callbacks, and after-hours charting
    are a top driver of staff/vet burnout; documentation eats clinical hours.
  Wedge: an ambient-capture + structured-record add-on that sits on top of the
    clinic's existing practice-management system (PIMS), not a PIMS replacement.

CANDIDATE 4 — Dental insurance-verification + denial follow-up (dental)
  Target user: independent dental offices (e.g. on Open Dental / Dentrix).
  Problem/manual task: front-desk/billing staff spend growing time verifying patient
    insurance benefits and chasing underpaid or denied claims.
  Wedge: automated eligibility/benefit checks and denial follow-up driven by
    payer-portal rules.

CANDIDATE 5 — Hourly / blue-collar high-volume applicant screening (recruiting)
  Target user: multi-location hourly employers (retail, QSR, warehouse, logistics).
  Problem/manual task: high-volume roles take longer to fill and recruiters are
    buried in large volumes of unqualified applicants to screen and schedule.
  Wedge: commodity screening/qualification + interview scheduling that plugs into
    existing low-cost applicant-tracking systems.

CANDIDATE 6 — Specialty / post-acute prior-auth + denial-appeal packets (healthcare RCM)
  Target user: a single specialty or post-acute segment (e.g. inpatient-rehab / LTCH)
    practices or facilities and their billing teams.
  Problem/manual task: clinicians/staff spend many hours per week on insurance
    prior-authorization submissions, and on assembling appeal packets for denials
    (many of which are overturned on appeal).
  Wedge: prior-auth submission + evidence-based denial-appeal packet generation for
    one payer/clinical segment.

CANDIDATE 7 — Schedule C client record-gathering for tax preparers (accounting/tax)
  Target user: small tax-preparation firms.
  Problem/manual task: most of the work is upstream recordkeeping, and chasing
    small-business (Schedule C) clients for missing documents before the deadline is
    manual and repetitive.
  Wedge: bank/card-feed categorization plus a client portal that proactively chases
    missing Schedule C records ahead of tax season.
