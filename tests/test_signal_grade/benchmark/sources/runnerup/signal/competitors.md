**Context evidence (not a competitor, but market-size proof, secondary/press):** HHS OIG
(2026-06) reports Medicare Advantage organizations denied 54% of IRF and 65% of LTCH prior-auth
requests on average, with the three largest MA insurers (UnitedHealth, Humana, CVS) denying
71-80% of LTCH/IRF requests; 36-43% of those denials were overturned when appealed — [HHS OIG
data brief](https://oig.hhs.gov/documents/audit/11693/OEI-09-24-00330.pdf), 2026-06. Separately,
UnitedHealth/naviHealth's "nH Predict" algorithm (the payer-side AI making these level-of-care
denial decisions) is the subject of a federal class-action alleging a ~90% error rate — secondary
press, multiple outlets, 2023-2026 reporting.

**Read:** thin-or-none, and I read it as **untapped rather than no-market**. Despite targeted
searching ("IRF," "LTCH," "appeal packet," "post-acute denial AI startup"), no named vendor sells
same-day admission-denial appeal packets to rehab hospitals on a contingency/revenue-share basis.
The adjacent categories are well-funded and real (Cohere Health $200M, Waystar public, Dext
acquired for ~£525M-adjacent category, Counterforce Health 10,000+ appeals processed for free) —
proving "AI fights insurer denials" is a validated, investable shape of product. But every comp
found either (a) serves outpatient claim denials, not institutional admission/level-of-care
denials (Muni, Claimable), (b) is a free consumer nonprofit that may not even cover Medicare
Advantage cases (Counterforce), or (c) is a generic enterprise RCM suite not specialized for
IRF/LTCH urgency or admission-specific criteria (Waystar, CombineHealth). The OIG-documented
54-65% denial rate and 36-43% overturn-on-appeal rate is strong, primary, government-sourced
evidence that the underlying pain is large and real — this is the strongest demand proxy found
for any candidate in this report, even though it comes with zero direct competitive pressure
visible.

**Confidence: medium** — the absence-of-competitor finding is well-supported by multiple
targeted searches (high confidence nothing obvious was missed), and the problem-size evidence is
primary/government-sourced (high confidence), but "no competitor found" can never be fully
confirmed via free search alone — a well-funded stealth entrant could exist without public
marketing.
