SHORTLIST SOURCE: the product role's owner-requested deep research on travel discovery by
preference (2026-10-03). Four versions of one idea, researched side by side. Only each version's
name, target user, problem, wedge and money model are given, deliberately WITHOUT stats or URLs:
gather evidence independently and do your own fresh research for every figure. Score each of the
four separately. US first, English sources; note big non-US players where they matter.

READ FIRST: V1-V3 are CONSUMER products and V4 is a business product. For V1-V3 the target user
IS the traveler: first-person complaints come from travelers themselves (travel forums that load
normally, Hacker News, the Stack Exchange travel site, and App Store reviews of the CONSUMER
trip-planning apps travelers use; for these versions the consumer app is the target user's app).
For V4 the target users are travel sellers (independent travel advisors, host agencies, tour
operators, hotel groups, destination marketing organisations): their words are in advisor
communities and in reviews of the software they work in. For V1-V3, people paid to do the task by
hand are those paid to plan trips or match travelers to destinations (travel advisors, trip
designers, concierge planners); consumers themselves do not post jobs.

PLAYERS TO CHECK (existence and overlap with each version, plus any others you find): Mindtrip,
Layla, Wanderlog, TripIt, Google (Gemini, AI Mode in Search, Google Flights Explore), ChatGPT and
any travel apps inside it, Expedia's AI assistant, Booking.com's AI trip planner, Trip.com
TripGenie, Kayak, Tripadvisor, Airbnb, Hopper, Polarsteps, GetYourGuide, Viator; for V4 also
Travefy, Tern and Fora.

V1 - AI-chat trip planner for leisure travelers (consumer)
  Target user: US leisure travelers planning a vacation (couples, families, friend groups).
  Problem: working out where to go and what to do takes hours across blogs, reviews, maps and
    booking sites, and generic top-10 lists ignore their tastes, budget and constraints.
  Wedge: a chat assistant that asks about preferences and constraints and returns a personal
    day-by-day itinerary (places, stays, activities on a map) with bookable links. Money: free to
    use, affiliate commissions on bookings, optional premium subscription (about $5-10 a month).

V2 - Preference quiz that matches travelers to destinations (consumer, or a widget for travel media and sellers)
  Target user: travelers who know their budget and dates but not where to go; second, travel
    media sites and travel sellers that would embed the quiz.
  Problem: choosing a destination among endless inspiration (where should we go this time?).
  Wedge: a short quiz (budget, dates, home airport, interests, travel style) that returns ranked
    destination matches with reasons, typical prices and best season. Money: affiliate links on a
    consumer site or app, or a widget licensed to travel media, tour operators or destination
    marketers (about $100-500 a month, or a revenue share).

V3 - Recommendations from a traveler's own past trips (consumer)
  Target user: frequent US leisure travelers (about 3 or more trips a year) and loyalty-program
    members.
  Problem: every plan starts from zero; travel tools do not know what the traveler liked before.
  Wedge: import past trips from email confirmations, loyalty accounts, photos or maps/location
    history; build a taste profile; recommend next destinations, stays and activities. Money:
    freemium subscription (about $5-10 a month or $50 a year) plus affiliate commissions.

V4 - Client-preference matching sold to travel businesses (business)
  Target user: independent travel advisors and host agencies first; then tour operators, hotel
    groups and destination marketing organisations.
  Problem: advisors spend hours learning each client's tastes and researching options before a
    proposal; destinations and hotels want well-matched visitors.
  Wedge: a client intake (quiz or chat) plus a matching engine that drafts a matched proposal for
    the advisor to edit (about $30-80 per advisor per month), or an embeddable matcher and
    qualified-lead source for destinations, tour operators and hotel groups (per lead or annual
    licence).
