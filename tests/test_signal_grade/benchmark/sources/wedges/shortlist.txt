Both briefs said "change the wedge": move from the front-end task, which practice software and payer
portals are bundling for free, to the back-end task (chasing denied and underpaid claims, appealing
denials). Below are the two NEW back-end wedges and, as CONTROLS, the two OLD front-end wedges, so the
run measures them side by side. Only each idea's name, target user, manual task and wedge are given,
deliberately WITHOUT the briefs' stats or URLs: gather demand evidence INDEPENDENTLY, so any agreement
is real triangulation, not an echo. Do your own fresh research for every figure. Score each of the
four separately; do not merge a new wedge with its control.

CANDIDATE A (NEW) — Dental denied/underpaid-claim follow-up for Open Dental offices (dental)
  Target user: independent single-office dental practices running Open Dental (owner dentist,
    office manager, insurance coordinator).
  Problem/manual task: insurance claims come back denied, stall unpaid for weeks, or get paid below
    the estimate; staff have no time to chase them, so money owed to the practice is never collected.
  Wedge: reads the office's claims through the Open Dental API, flags claims unpaid too long or paid
    below the estimate, checks their status electronically, and gives staff a ranked follow-up work
    list (target price about $199 per office per month).

CANDIDATE A0 (CONTROL, OLD) — Dental insurance-eligibility verification before the visit (dental)
  Target user: the same independent dental practices.
  Problem/manual task: before each appointment staff phone insurers or log into payer portals to
    check coverage, frequencies and remaining benefits.
  Wedge: automated eligibility and benefits verification ahead of each appointment.

CANDIDATE B (NEW) — Medicare Advantage admission-denial appeal packets for rehab hospitals (post-acute care)
  Target user: freestanding inpatient rehabilitation facilities (IRFs) first, then long-term care
    hospitals (LTCHs): admissions directors, utilization-review and billing leaders.
  Problem/manual task: Medicare Advantage plans deny admission requests; appealing takes hours of
    record-pulling and letter-writing within tight deadlines, so many denials are never appealed and
    the admission (and its revenue) is lost.
  Wedge: same-day appeal packets for denied admissions (records pulled, payer criteria matched,
    letter drafted), priced as a share of each admission won back.

CANDIDATE B0 (CONTROL, OLD) — Prior-authorization submission and tracking for post-acute providers (post-acute care)
  Target user: the same rehab and long-term care hospitals.
  Problem/manual task: staff submit prior-authorization requests through payer portals and fax,
    then chase their status by phone.
  Wedge: automated prior-authorization submission and status tracking.
