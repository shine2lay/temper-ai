## RECOMMENDATION

**A critical tension to surface, not average away:** A0 ranks #1 almost entirely on
the strength of its **jobs** (3.0) and **competitors** (2.4) sub-scores. But the
competitors evidence behind that 2.4 is a mix of two very different things the 0-3
scale can't separate: a *paid, funded, currently-growing point solution* (Zuub,
Series A 2024, 4.7/5 on 38 reviews) **and** *incumbent PMS vendors bundling the exact
same feature for free* (Dentrix "Eligibility Offerings," Curve Dental "Eligibility+")
plus a listicle flatly calling it "baseline... not a differentiator." That free-
bundling pattern is precisely the commoditization risk the original opportunity
brief cited as its reason to deprioritize this front-end wedge — and this run found
it independently, without being told the brief's thesis, which is real triangulation
of that specific risk. Zuub's traction shows *some* room still exists for a better
paid tool alongside the free baseline, but nothing in the evidence shows whether a
*new* entrant could repeat that, specifically.

**Recommend advancing A0 (top score) and B (strongest NEW-wedge case) to Rung 1,
both labeled promising-but-unproven:**

- **A0** — strongest mechanical case: a measured, high-confidence job market
  (6,129 exact-title postings, $16-32/hr) is real evidence of budget, and Zuub
  proves *someone* pays for this standalone. **Biggest unknown:** whether a new
  entrant can out-sell both the free PMS-bundled baseline and Zuub. **Cheap check
  before spending more:** interview 10-15 independent practices on willingness to
  pay a premium over whatever's already free in their existing PMS — if that's "no,"
  this candidate is dead regardless of its score.
- **B** — notably **outranks its own control, B0** (1.12 vs. 0.94), despite a
  thinner job-board count, because its problem evidence is unusually strong and
  convergent across three independent sources (AMRPA 57.4% denial rate, HHS OIG 43%
  IRF overturn, KFF 54% IRF denial rate vs. <8% overall) and its competitive
  landscape shows a real gap (no IRF/LTCH-specific vendor) sitting inside a funded,
  validated adjacent category with a documented weak incumbent (Waystar's recurring
  1-star support complaints) to wedge against. **Biggest unknown:** zero pain-voice
  was found anywhere — but the pain agent attributes this to a structural gap (no
  accessible IRF/LTCH forum existed in the venue list), not disinterest. **Cheap
  check:** directly interview 5-10 IRF/LTCH UR/admissions directors — a channel this
  desk research simply could not reach — to get real first-person pain-voice before
  spending on build.

**Drop / deprioritize: Candidate A** (lowest score, 0.89) — weakest or thinnest on
every signal: no measured search volume, job-board evidence diluted by broad
titles, only 2 off-target tool-gripe pain quotes (not the "no time to chase claims"
statement the wedge needs), and a competitive landscape too thin to tell whether it
is genuine whitespace or a dead niche. Not proven dead, but the weakest case to
spend on next without a cheap pain-interview check first.

**B0 is a near-drop too:** it sits just above A (0.94) specifically because, unlike
B, its three named competitors (Rhyme $56.9M, Silna Health $27.1M) are already
well-funded **direct** competitors for the literal task, with no documented weak
point and no confirmed IRF/LTCH-specific posting — a tougher, more contested wedge
than B's "crowded one layer up, open at the specific niche" position.

## LIMITATIONS

- **This is Rung 0 desk evidence (words/proxies gathered via WebSearch/WebFetch),
  not real paid behavior.** Nothing here is a signed contract, a paid pilot, or even
  a cold-outreach response — it is evidence that the *problem* and *adjacent paid
  roles/products* exist, not that anyone will pay *this* product *this* price.
- **Tool/venue access gaps mean several "0" or "low" scores may reflect blocked
  channels, not true absence.** Google Trends 429'd on every attempt (search.md);
  Reddit returned zero WebSearch results across all four candidates (pain.md,
  search.md); G2 403'd on every URL attempted for three of the four candidates
  (competitors.md — A, B, B0); Indeed 403'd on direct fetch for every candidate
  (jobs.md), forcing reliance on WebSearch-extracted estimates. The pain-voice
  signal is especially fragile for B/B0: the agent states plainly that no
  IRF/LTCH-specific forum exists in the accessible venue list, so pain=0 there
  is a coverage gap, not evidence the target users are untroubled.
- **Shakiest scores:**
  - A0's **search** score was explicitly downgraded from the agent's stated "med"
    to "low" because every input was vendor PR or listicle — per the evidence-
    discipline rule, this must not be hidden behind the agent's own label.
  - Candidate A's and B0's **competitors** funding figures rely on secondary
    aggregators that disagree with each other (Zentist: Tracxn vs. CBInsights vs.
    getlatka give different totals; Rhyme: only a secondary-aggregator summary,
    direct Crunchbase/PitchBook blocked) — treat these dollar figures as
    directional, not precise.
  - All four candidates' **pain** score of 0-1 rests on a single or zero venues;
    this is the thinnest signal across the whole board and the one most likely to
    change with direct outreach (the cheap checks recommended above target exactly
    this gap for the two advancing candidates).
